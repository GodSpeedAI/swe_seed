# Research Prompt Packet

- Prompt ID: prompt.agentic_dev_purchase_behavior
- Strategy Option: option.agentic_dev_harness

## Objective

- Determine whether target users already spend money on comparable or adjacent solutions related to proof-backed AI development workflows.

## Questions

- What paid tools or services already improve AI-assisted development reliability?
- Which solutions are direct, adjacent, or analogical comparables?
- What price anchors are visible?
- What evidence weakens the case that users would pay for a separate harness?

## Anti-Bias Instructions

- Do not search only for supporting evidence.
- Actively look for contradictory evidence.
- Separate facts from interpretation.
